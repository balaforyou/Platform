Breakpoints
Mobile · under 600px
One column, as drawn. Slot grid 3 across. Sticky pay bar. Content max 390–430px.
Tablet · 600–1024px
Still one column, centred at 560px. Slot grid 4–5 across. Pay bar stops being sticky and sits after the review card.
Laptop · 1024px and up
Two columns: picker left, review and pay in a sticky right rail at 360px. Slot grid 6 across, one row per period.
Carry into the code
44px is the floor on every pointer, not only touch — a laptop with a touchscreen reports both. Gate the growth on @media (pointer: coarse) and keep the 44px hit area on fine pointers too.
Respect env(safe-area-inset-bottom) on the sticky bar, or the pay button sits under the home indicator when the PWA is installed.
Focus ring is a 2px accent outline at 2px offset on every control — keyboard users on laptop get the same flow.
Scrolling
After picking a slot
Duration, court and review appear below the fold. Scroll only far enough to reveal the duration stepper — keep the slot grid on screen or people lose their place.
After changing day or period
Do not scroll. The grid reloads in place and the user is already looking at it.
OTP field on mobile
The keyboard covers the verify button. Lift the field above it on focus.
Everywhere
Give scroll targets a scroll-margin-top so they don't land flush under the sticky header, and gate smooth behaviour on prefers-reduced-motion.
