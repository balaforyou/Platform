TWO THINGS TO DECIDE IN CODE
The cancel modal's close button is 34px in CancelBookingModal.tsx (p-1.5 on a 16px icon) — it is 44px here, so fix it in code rather than matching the old markup. The identical band means Pay, OTP and Venue info lose their in-band back arrow — it becomes a pill in the page body. Check that against your e2e specs, which target the band's button today. And generateAccentRamp is only wired for --color-accent-*; once brand-primary is an alias, the pre-resolve fallback in :root is the only thing standing between a slow tenant fetch and an unstyled first paint.
